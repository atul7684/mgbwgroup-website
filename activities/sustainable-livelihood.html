Sustainable Livelihood and Women Empowerment

Creating opportunities for economic empowerment

  • We focus on generating sustainable livelihoods through skill development, community engagement, and support for micro-enterprises.
  • Our programs encourage small business promotion by connecting individuals with markets, finance, and training.
  • We provide self-employment training for women, equipping them with practical skills to enhance their employability and confidence.
  • Over 80,000 women have been empowered by our employment initiatives, with many earning between ₹10,000 to ₹25,000 per month.
  • These efforts aim to build economic independence and drive inclusive development across regions.
