10 CORE PRINCIPLES OF CSR AT MGBW GROUP

Our core CSR priciples drive us to implement effective initiatives and create a positive impact in the society.

01
01

Leading with Purpose

We don’t stop at compliance — we aim to set new benchmarks in corporate responsibility. Our initiatives are designed to be pioneering, scalable, and transformative, ensuring that education, healthcare, and livelihoods are not only accessible but also equitable, inclusive, and future-ready. We aspire to inspire other organizations to raise the bar in social impact.

02
02

Impactful

Interventions focus on creating tangible impact - from empowering 80,000 women with sustainable livelihoods earning ₹10,000-₹25,000 monthly to establishing comprehensive healthcare infrastructure in underserved regions.

03
03

Linked to Business

All CSR activities leverage our organizational strengths and expertise while ensuring community benefits remain paramount. Our programs align business capabilities with social development needs for maximum effectiveness.

04
04

Relevant to National & Local Contexts

CSR initiatives are deeply aligned with local contexts, promoting Kannada literature, Yakshagana performances, and Uttara Kannada folk culture while contributing to national integration and regional heritage preservation..

05
05

Sustainable Development Principles

All interventions follow sustainable development principles, factoring social equity, human rights, gender inclusivity, and environmental impacts in design and execution across education, healthcare, and livelihood programs.

06
06

Participative & Bottom-up

Special emphasis on reaching underprivileged communities, rural populations, women entrepreneurs, and marginalized groups. Programs specifically target gender equity, rural healthcare access, and educational opportunities for underserved areas.

07
07

Focused on the Disadvantaged

Special emphasis on reaching underprivileged communities, rural populations, women entrepreneurs, and marginalized groups. Programs specifically target gender equity, rural healthcare access, and educational opportunities for underserved areas.

08
08

Strategic & Built to Last

All CSR initiatives are designed to ensure communities can sustain programs beyond our direct involvement. Infrastructure development, skill training, and institutional partnerships create lasting developmental foundations.

09
09

Partnerships

All interventions are implemented through strategic partnerships with NGOs, local authorities, government institutions, cultural organizations, and community groups to ensure ground-level effectiveness and sustainability.

10
10

Opportunities for Volunteering

CSR initiatives provide diverse volunteering opportunities for employees - from educational mentoring and healthcare camps to cultural event organization and women's skill development program facilitation.
