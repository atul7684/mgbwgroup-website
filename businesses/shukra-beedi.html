Shukra Beedies LLP

Incorporated in November 1982, Shukra Beedies Limited Liability Partnership was established with a clear vision—to manufacture high-quality beedies with consistency and integrity. With over four decades of operational experience, we have established a trusted reputation among both consumers and distributors.

Backed by traditional expertise and operational discipline, Shukra Beedies continues to cater to regional and national markets, delivering reliable volumes with precision.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Decades of focused manufacturing
  • Clean and compliant production units
  • Steady supply chain practices
  • Skilled labor backed by experienced supervision
  • Efficient product packaging & labeling

Production Capability

Shukra Beedies operates with a focus on efficiency and output quality, employing skilled workers and adhering to structured processes. Our facilities emphasize:

  • Product uniformity
  • Tendu leaf quality checks
  • Humidity-controlled storage
  • Timely dispatches for local and regional distribution

Market Presence

While Shukra Beedies primarily serves domestic markets, our infrastructure and systems are ready to scale toward international exports and white-label distribution as well. Our goal is to build strong partnerships with established networks and regional wholesalers across India and beyond.

Why Partner with Us

  • Over 40 years of industry presence
  • Professional setup with reliable compliance
  • Clear pricing structures and supply timelines
  • Responsive distributor support
  • Packaging adaptability for bulk markets

Partner With Us

We are actively seeking committed partners who value long-term relationships, steady demand, and a brand built on consistency. Whether you're expanding an existing network or entering the market, Shukra Beedies offers a dependable and transparent partnership.
