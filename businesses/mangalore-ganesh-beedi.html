About Us

●

Scaling Success

Mangalore Ganesh Beedi Works has been a symbol of quality and tradition for more than eight decades. Renowned for its flagship brand, “501 Mangalore Ganesh Beedi”, the company has built a strong presence in the domestic market and grown into a recognized name internationally. Our operations support thousands of skilled artisans who handcraft beedis without the use of chemicals, preserving authenticity and craftsmanship that stand the test of time. This commitment to quality and traditional methods has been the foundation of our enduring success.

From its humble beginnings in Mangalore, MGBW has evolved into one of India’s leading beedi manufacturers, with products now exported to several countries across the globe. Over the years, we have earned national and international recognition for product excellence, ethical business practices, and contributions to employment generation. Guided by innovation, traditional values, and customer trust, Mangalore Ganesh Beedi Works is well-positioned for continued growth and industry leadership in the years to come.

The Beedi That Built the Brand

History of 501 Manglore Ganesh Beedi Works

Mangalore Ganesh Beedi Works (MGBW), incorporated in 1940, is headquartered in Mysuru, India. As one of the oldest and most respected beedi manufacturers in the country, MGBW is renowned for its commitment to producing high-quality, expertly packed beedis crafted from premium tobacco blends. Each product adheres to the highest industry standards and technical specifications, made possible through a state-of-the-art production facility dedicated to processing and refining the finest tobacco.

The brand commands a strong presence in both domestic and international markets. MGBW is among the select few companies in India licensed to manufacture beedis, a testament to its regulatory compliance and industry standing. Its products have received global recognition for excellence.

Evolution of Leadership

Founded in 1940 by Sri Bantwal Raghuram Prabhu and Sri Madhav Shenoi in the peaceful village of Bantwal, South Canara district of Karnataka, Mangalore Ganesh Beedi Works (MGBW) has evolved into one of India’s most iconic and respected beedi manufacturers. What began as a small-scale initiative rooted in traditional craftsmanship has grown into a nationally recognized brand, prominently known for its flagship product, 501 Ganesh Beedi.

Today, the company is under the capable leadership of Mr. Jagannath Shenoi (Managing Partner) and Mr. Govind Shenoy (Executive Partner), who equally share responsibilities in driving the business forward. MGBW operates multiple strategically located manufacturing units across Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and Telangana.

With more than eight decades, MGBW remains committed to its core values of quality, tradition, and customer satisfaction, while embracing innovation and sustainable business practices to stay ahead in the competitive market.
