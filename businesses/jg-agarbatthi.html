About Us

●

A Tradition of Aromatic Excellence

J&G Agarbatti has emerged as a leading name in incense stick production and distribution across India & Abroad. We combine time-honored craftsmanship with modern practices to deliver superior-quality incense that enriches spiritual rituals and daily environments.

Guided by our values of purity, sustainability, and devotion, we use only the finest authentic elements, creating signature blends that are both calming and enduring.

●

What Makes Us Different

●

Rooted in Tradition. Crafted with Devotion

  • Use of natural resins, essential oils, and eco-friendly bases
  • Fragrances inspired by Indian tradition and spiritual needs
  • Consistent burn time and rich aromatic throw
  • Sustainable production and packaging
  • Compliance with national quality standards
●

Product Experience

●

Designed for Every Sacred Space

J&G incense products are designed to complement every setting, from homes and prayer rooms to temples and meditation centers. Our catalogue includes traditional florals, woody blends, calming earthy tones, and premium variants for spiritual and commercial use.

  • Regular Incense Sticks
  • Premium Long-Lasting Series
  • Ritual and Festival Collections

Market Reach & Distribution

●

Presence Across India. Ready to Expand Further.

With a strong distribution network across the Indian Subcontinent, J&G Agarbatti has developed a long-standing relationship with wholesalers, spiritual supply stores, supermarkets, and temple trusts. We are now actively expanding into new regional markets and other countries with dedicated support for our partners.
