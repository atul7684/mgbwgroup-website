Privacy Policy

At MGBW Group, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or interact with our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use our website:

  • Personal details (such as name, email address, phone number) when you submit inquiries, contact forms, or partnership requests.
  • Business information if you apply to join our distributor or partner network.
  • Technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and browsing data) through cookies or analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and requests.
  • Process distributor or partnership applications.
  • Share updates about our services, businesses, or events (if you opt in).
  • Improve the performance and functionality of our website.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. How We Protect Your Information

We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share limited data only with:

  • Service providers who assist us in operating our website or business.
  • Regulatory authorities if required by law.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and analytics tools to enhance your browsing experience. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites (such as Silent Shores, MGBW Garage’s Instagram, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties and encourage you to review their policies.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask for corrections or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent from receiving communications.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at: mgbwworks@gmail.com

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
