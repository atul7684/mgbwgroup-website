The MGBW Group

With over eight decades of history, MGBW Group is a family-driven enterprise built on trust, tradition, and principled growth. What began as a modest vision has evolved into a diversified organization with a wide-reaching impact across industries and generations. Our journey is rooted in a deep commitment to ethical business practices, long-term relationships, and meaningful contributions to the communities we serve. Guided by the underlying principles of our founder, we continue to build with purpose, lead with integrity, and create value that stands the test of time.

Our Businesses

●

Mangalore Ganesh Beedi Works

Established in 1940, Mangalore Ganesh Beedi is a trusted name in India's beedi industry, renowned for its heritage of quality and consistency. Our flagship 501 Ganesh Beedi continues to serve millions, with growing reach across domestic and global markets.

Explore More

Shukra Beedies LLP

Incorporated in 1982, Shukra Beedies LLP brings tradition and precision together to deliver consistent quality and trusted craftsmanship. With a strong foundation and a focus on responsible manufacturing, we continue to serve evolving markets with integrity.

Explore More

J&G Agarbatti

Blending natural ingredients with traditional craftsmanship, J&G Agarbatti creates premium incense products that elevate every space. With a commitment to quality and sustainability, we bring fragrance to homes, temples, and hearts across India.

Explore More

Silent Shores Resorts & Spa

Nestled by a tranquil lake in Mysuru, Silent Shores is a luxury resort offering serene stays, holistic wellness, and curated experiences. Whether for leisure, celebrations, or rejuvenation, it's where calm meets comfort.

Explore More

MGBW Garage

Fueled by speed, powered by passion. MGBW Garage dives into the world of high-performance machines, motorsport vibes, and adrenaline-fueled builds. It's not just about cars—it's a lifestyle on wheels. From rare customs to precision detailing, every story is built for those who live fast, think bold, and drive louder than words.

501 Safety Matches

Introduced in August 2025, 501 Safety Matches extend the trusted promise of MGBW Group into a complementary product line for Ganesh Beedi, Shukra Beedi, and J&G Agarbatti. Designed for everyday convenience and reliability, they carry forward the same commitment to quality and consistency that defines all our brands.

Contact Us

Shukra Deepam Oil

Shukra Deepam Oil blends rice bran, sesame, castor, and coconut oils with cow ghee for pooja and lamp lighting. Rooted in tradition, it’s a thoughtful choice for daily worship and special occasions, bringing an auspicious touch to every prayer.
