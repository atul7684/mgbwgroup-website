About MGBW Group

Pioneering excellence across diversified sectors with a tradition of trust since 1940.

Eight Decades.One Journey. Many Futures

MGBW Group is a family-led, values-driven enterprise built over eight decades of growth and trust. Established in 1940 with the founding of Mangalore Ganesh Beedi Works by Shri Bantwal Raghuram Prabhu & Shri Madhav Shenoi, the Group has steadily evolved across sectors, ranging from tobacco and wellness to hospitality, fragrance, and automotive lifestyle.

Guided by discipline, purpose, and people-first thinking, the MGBW Group’s foundations are rooted in trust and ethical growth. What began as a humble venture has grown into a multi-vertical enterprise that continues to touch millions of lives, led today by Shri DR. M Jagannath Shenoi and Shri Govind J. Shenoy, who bring generational vision and stewardship to the business. Their leadership reflects a seamless blend of tradition and innovation, carrying forward the founder’s principles while embracing modern aspirations.

At its core, MGBW stands not just for business excellence, but for building institutions that endure.

Our Vision

To be a progressive and diversified conglomerate that blends our heritage with innovation, creating lasting value across tobacco, FMCG, hospitality, and allied businesses — while steadfastly upholding The Tradition of Trust since 1940.

Mission

To uphold the Tradition of Trust by ensuring uncompromising quality and authenticity in every product and service.

  • To drive sustainable growth through diversification into FMCG, hospitality, and allied sectors with a strong customer-centric approach.
  • To foster a culture of innovation, operational excellence, and environmental responsibility across all businesses.
  • To empower employees, partners, and communities through fair practices, inclusive opportunities, and shared prosperity.
  • To actively contribute to cultural, educational, and social initiatives, reinforcing our commitment to community welfare and heritage preservation.

Carrying Forward the Tradition with Vision

●

M. Govind Rao

M. Govind Rao was a visionary industrialist and humanitarian whose life reflected humility, discipline, and compassion. A key pillar of Mangalore Ganesh Beedi, he played a significant role in expanding the business while upholding ethics, transparency, and respect for employees.

Known for his simplicity despite great success, he believed wealth was meaningful only when used for the upliftment of society.

A patriotic soul, he generously contributed to national causes and supported numerous social initiatives without seeking recognition. His leadership style focused on empowerment rather than charity, and his life continues to inspire as a model of integrity, service, and purposeful living.

DR. M Jagannath Shenoi

Managing Partner, Mangalore Ganesh Beedi Works
Chairman, Silent Shores Resorts & Spa

Dr. M. Jagannath Shenoi carries forward the tradition of the Shenoi family with a rare blend of business leadership and philanthropy. Taking charge of Mangalore Ganesh Beedi Works in the mid-1970s after the untimely loss of his father, he transformed challenges into opportunities, strengthening the company while fostering an environment where workers could progress into entrepreneurs themselves.

Beyond business, Dr. Jagannath Shenoi has dedicated his life to education, healthcare, and community service. He established the Jnanavikasa Kendra to provide free education for underprivileged children, serves as a trustee of Kamakshi Hospital, and has led numerous cultural and educational trusts in Mysuru. His contributions have earned him prestigious recognitions, including the Karnataka Kanmani Award, the Manava Ratna Award, and an Honorary Doctorate in Business Management.

Guided by compassion, faith, and integrity, Dr. Jagannath Shenoi exemplifies the values on which the MGBW Group was founded, ensuring its growth is always intertwined with social good.

Govind J. Shenoy

Executive Partner, MGBW Group

Mr. Govind J. Shenoy represents the new generation of leadership at MGBW Group, combining a value-driven outlook with a modern, global perspective. He entered the family business at the age of 23 and has since been instrumental in strengthening its core operations while expanding into new sectors.

His business interests span across diverse sectors, serving as Director of Kaynes Hotels Pvt Ltd, which operates the renowned Silent Shores Resort & Spa, and as Managing Partner of J&G, a leading name in premium incense sticks. His forward-thinking approach has also extended to digital-first ventures such as MGBW Garage, positioning the Group for relevance in new markets.

Beyond his role within the Group, Mr. Govind Shenoy serves as Vice President of the All India Beedi Industry Federation (AIBIF), where he actively contributes to the industry’s advocacy and policy direction. With a leadership style rooted in tradition yet oriented toward innovation, he continues to carry forward the Group’s vision of building enterprises with purpose, integrity, and long-term impact.
